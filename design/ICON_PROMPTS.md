Common prompt constraints: one square Android launcher icon for RoseVault; grey lock symbol; monochrome grey only; generous safe area; readable at small sizes; no pink, letter R, text, numbers, watermark, or phone mockup.

1. Minimal: full-bleed cool medium-grey background, centered pale-silver closed padlock with bold rounded shape and simple keyhole; clean flat graphic.
2. Metallic: full-bleed charcoal background with subtle lighting, centered brushed-silver closed padlock, restrained rounded bevels and a small keyhole.
3. Selected shield: full-bleed light stone-grey background, centered dark graphite shield containing a silver-grey closed padlock; balanced geometric shapes with soft depth.

Generated originals were copied into this project. The adaptive launcher uses the selected raster with safe-area insets and a matching grey background.
